How I work

Every analysis follows the same four steps. The last one is where data becomes a decision.

  1. Ask

    Understand the business question and what decision the data should support.

  2. Clean

    Collect the data, fix missing values and duplicates, and check quality.

  3. Analyse

    Explore with Python, SQL and Excel to find patterns, trends and answers.

  4. Share

    Present the findings in a clear dashboard or report, then refine with feedback.
